        Connect Apple Health

        Apple Health has to push data to this dashboard — there's no system-level toggle Claude can flip for you. Here's the one-time setup.

        1. On your iPhone, install Health Auto Export – JSON+CSV from the App Store.
        2. Open it, grant Health permissions, then create two automations under Automations → New Automation.
        3. Automation 1: choose the metrics data type with Steps, Active Energy, Resting Heart Rate, HRV, Sleep Analysis.
        4. Automation 2: choose Workouts.
        5. For each automation, set Frequency to Daily, Format to JSON, and Destination to REST API with URL: https://admins-mac-mini.tail6c6490.ts.net:5050/api/apple-health/sync
        6. Save each automation. To backfill or test, tap Manual Export, confirm the date range, then tap Begin Export.

        Once the first sync lands, this card will flip to "Synced today" automatically.
