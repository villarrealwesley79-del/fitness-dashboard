FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need an Oura Ring?
No. Manual workout logging, body tracking, soreness tracking, and nutrition logging all work without an Oura Ring. Oura integration is a Pro feature that adds recovery-aware recommendations. If you don't have an Oura, you can still use FitOS to track everything — you just manually rate your recovery each day.
How is this different from Strong or JEFIT?
Strong and JEFIT are great for logging sets and reps. FitOS does that too, but it also layers in recovery intelligence — using your Oura data to tell you when to push hard vs. when to pull back. Most apps tell you what to do; FitOS tells you what to do based on how your body responded last night.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Your data is yours. Full JSON export available from your settings page at any time. We don't hold your data hostage.
What happens after my 7-day trial?
You'll be prompted to enter payment to continue with Pro features. If you don't upgrade, you keep access to basic logging only (no Oura integration, no smart recommendations). No automatic charges — you choose when to subscribe.
Is this available on mobile?
FitOS is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Open it in Safari/Chrome on your phone, tap "Add to Home Screen," and it works like a native app — offline-capable and full-screen. No App Store download required.
How secure is my health data?
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Your Oura token is stored encrypted at rest. We never sell your data. Health data is yours — not a product.
